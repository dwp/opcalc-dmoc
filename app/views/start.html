OpCalc

Use OpCalc to work out a customer's benefit entitlement based on their income, savings and household circumstances.

Use this service to:

  • calculate benefit overpayments, underpayments and arrears accurately across a wide range of benefit products
  • determine the correct benefit entitlement based on customer circumstances
  • generate supporting forms and calculation outputs to help evidence decisions and manage benefit discrepancies
