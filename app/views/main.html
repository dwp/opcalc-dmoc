{# app/views/layouts/main.html Every page in the prototype extends this file, which is why one missing wrapper here showed up as all 60 screens rendering flush to the left edge with a sliver of horizontal scroll. What was happening: govuk-grid-row carries margin-left: -15px and margin-right: -15px. Those negative margins are meant to be absorbed by the 15px padding on govuk-width-container. With no width container around the content, nothing absorbed them - so every page started at 0 instead of 15px and ran 15px past the right edge of the window, and nothing capped the content at the 1020px GOV.UK maximum. Rather than rebuild the page skeleton by hand, this extends the Prototype Kit's own layout.
